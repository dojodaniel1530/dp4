98.7 KDJO — San Diego Internet Radio

KDJO 98.7 is an internet-only radio station (not an FM broadcast) out of San Diego, streaming every day from 6 AM to 2 AM. It’s my own creation and totally independent, so that means I need money :/ The stream itself has no ad breaks (the only ads are the small banners on this website), and the music comes from independent artists that my team and I think are neat. We hand-select rotations spanning surf rock, indie pop, underground hip-hop, vaporwave, and more.

We’re also the exclusive home of Left On Base for general yap about Padres baseball, San Diego FC, and SDSU/UCSD college sports. Hit play up top to listen live, or scroll down to check out our schedule and DJ archives. If you wanna add some songs, feel free to use the contact form below—Spotify playlists are ideal, but I’ll take anything! Just keep in mind all shows and times are subject to change based on vibes.
